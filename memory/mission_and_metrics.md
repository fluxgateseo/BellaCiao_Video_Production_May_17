Bellaciao.ai is an AI receptionist for restaurants and hospitality businesses. The script engine's job is to convert restaurant owners in **Australia, the USA, and the UK** into demo bookings.

- **Primary metric**: Demo bookings + trial activations per week
- **Secondary metric**: DM trigger keywords ("BELLA" / "TRIAL" / "DEMO") per week
- **Channel-health proxies (NOT goals)**: 100k IG followers, 10k YT subs in 6 months
- **Game**: Authority Building (primary) + Explanatory Product (secondary)
- **Outlier criteria**: saves rate >3% OR DM trigger activated — NEVER view count alone
- **Cities**: New York · London · Melbourne ONLY (no other cities, ever)

**Why:** The whole pipeline was built around restaurant-owner conversion, not generic engagement. View counts are vanity; saves and DMs are the real signal that a script earned a share-with-co-owner moment.

**How to apply:** When evaluating a script choice (friend, pillar, hook, ending), ask "would this restaurant owner share this at 11 PM without writing a caption?" If a request optimises for views, virality, or non-restaurant audiences, push back. If a friend or setting outside NY/London/Melbourne is suggested, reject it — this is a hard rule in brand.py.
