## The spec (from the Day 1 final cut + its review notes)
- **Format:** 9:16, **1080×1920**, **30 fps**, **~58s** full reel. (Stories are shorter.)
- **Voiced + lip-synced.** Built multi-shot: Shot 1 locked + **Veo 3.1 voiced**
  Shots 2–9. Bella speaks to camera; the VO carries the story.
- **Brand voice:** planned swap to the locked voice (the review note says Kling
  `commercial_lady_en_f-v1`; per pivot T4 the canonical voice is ElevenLabs
  `9DxYbhovJvj6zRtn0Pvc` — use that).
- **Burned captions:** 2 short lines, lower third, white (CapCut overlays, e.g.
  the old "Not the food." / "It's Google." — reframe the copy to calls/bookings).
- **Sign-off:** "I'm Bella — ciao for now." (spoken + on-screen).

## The visual language (carry this forward)
1. **Cinematic, filmic, warm.** Moody practical lighting (pendant lamps),
   shallow depth of field, gentle grain, rich warm grade. NOT flat or bright.
2. **Multi-shot narrative**, not a single talking head: alternate Bella
   to-camera shots with **B-roll of the friend in their real venue** (Maria at
   the empty checkered-cloth table; the empty four-top; hands, food, the room).
3. **Bella in the friend's actual venue** — medium close-up, centered, direct to
   lens, navy/dark top, gold hoops, dark waved hair, blue eyes (identity-locked).
4. **Signature device — Bella holds up a phone showing the on-screen "evidence."**
   In the GBP era it showed a Google Maps listing. **v2 adaptation: the screen
   shows the calls/bookings evidence instead** — e.g. "12 missed calls", a phone
   ringing out, an after-hours voicemail, OpenTable cover-fee invoice, or the
   Ciao booking confirmation. NEVER a Google/SEO screen.
5. **Ciao (the Italian Greyhound) appears at the turning point** — exactly once,
   never decorative (e.g. sitting beside Maria as the room sits empty).
6. **Real venue grounding** — specific neighbourhood, real details (Lygon St,
   checkered cloths, the regulars' table).
